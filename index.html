House Rules

Please take a moment to review these guidelines and help us keep the apartment safe and comfortable for everyone.

🚭 Safety & prohibited items

  • •No smoking. A fine will be deducted from the deposit.
  • •No pets or illegal items. Parties and drugs are forbidden.
  • •Please do not cook foods that produce strong odors or smoke, or pose a fire hazard, in the apartment kitchen.
  • •Please do not bring strong-smelling foods, such as durian, jackfruit, or fermented fish sauces, into the apartment.

🌙 Noise & visitors

  • •Please keep noise to a minimum after 10:00 pm. Bass speakers and karaoke equipment are not allowed.
  • •Check in with the number of guests stated in your booking. Please let us know in advance if you expect visitors.

🏠 Take care of the apartment

  • •Please take care of the room, its belongings, and facilities. Only non-tracing adhesive tape may be used for decoration.
  • •Do not remove any items from the property. You may be charged for damaged or missing items.
  • •Please use towels only for their intended purpose—not as a mat or mop.

✅ Before you leave

  • •Turn off the power when you leave and please recycle.
  • •Housekeeping is not provided during your stay. The service is available for an additional charge.
  • •We are not responsible for valuables or personal belongings. Please check that you have everything before leaving.
